Marketers who are triple checking how their AI tools interact with PII are asking the right questions, and they should keep asking them of every vendor who wants their data anywhere near an AI model. The past two years have given us a long list of reasons why, from employees pasting customer records into chat tools that retain and train on whatever gets typed in, to vendors bolting AI features onto existing products without checking what data those features actually touch.
